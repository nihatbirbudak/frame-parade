# Output quality checks

Record the actual file, checks performed, failures and any inspection you could not perform. A script exit code or generated report alone does not establish that a video is ready.

## Initial silent sample

Expected default output: projeler/ornek-dikey/cikti/tam.mp4, with tam.render.json beside it.

- The process completed successfully and the actual MP4 exists and is nonempty.
- The sample is portrait 1080×1920, 30 fps, 10 seconds. The expected render report values are boyut=1080x1920, kare=300, video_sn=10 and sesli=false.
- The channel name and selected language are correct; no placeholder channel or series name remains.
- Text is readable and stays within the frame. Fonts and graphics load correctly. There is no accidental clipping, blank scene or frozen transition.
- Playback matches the intended scene sequence. With no tam.wav, the output has a silent AAC track; this is expected.
- The owner can open the local preview and review it. If the assistant cannot inspect playback, say so and leave the visual result pending.
- No upload, publication, paid voice generation or unselected recurring task was used to obtain the sample.

## Later content

Check the title, opening text and opening narration against the same concrete topic and promise. The finished video must deliver what the title suggests.

Verify factual claims and calculations against sources. Label examples and estimates honestly. Read every final caption and on-screen text for spelling, meaning, timing, overflow and language consistency.

Inspect the actual animation and cuts for pacing and visual continuity. Review representative frames and transitions, including the beginning and end. Use only existing documented measurement tools; do not invent a pass result.

Listen to the final audio or have the owner review it. Check pronunciation, intelligibility, clipping, gaps and whether music/effects obscure speech. Unheard audio remains unverified. Keep use rights and selected voice/effect approval recorded.

## Before any approved upload

Present the actual final file, title, description, subtitles, thumbnail where applicable, destination channel, visibility or schedule, timezone and relevant declarations. The owner must choose the appropriate made-for-kids and synthetic-media declarations for that content.

Run the uploader's dry run without --yes only for a complete prepared package. Resolve failures and identify any manual Studio work. A clean dry run does not authorize sending.

Proceed with a real upload only after explicit approval covering that package and destination. Preserve the receipt and inspect a partial result before retrying. Do not describe a planned or private video as publicly available.

## Completion record

Mark each required check as passed, failed or not inspected, with a short reason. Record unresolved work and the user's review status in the project's management records. No promised view count, retention rate or algorithmic outcome is a quality check.
